The idea

A social deduction game for 4 to 12 players on one phone. Everyone gets the same secret word, except one player who's "out of the loop" (برا السالفة) and only knows the category.

How a round works

  1. Pass the phone. Each player taps to privately see either the word, or "You're out of the loop!"
  2. Ask around. Players take turns asking each other questions about the word. Answers should be vague enough to fool the outsider but specific enough to prove you know it.
  3. Vote. Everyone points at who they think the outsider is.
  4. Last chance. If the outsider is caught, they can still win by guessing the word from a list of options.

Planned extras

Arabic and English word packs (food, places, football, jobs, and more), two outsiders for big groups, a round timer, and scoring across rounds.
